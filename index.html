Our Story

Rooted in the Mountains of Feytroun

For nearly four decades, El Balady has been a cherished name in the heart of Feytroun, Lebanon. What started as a humble dairy shop in 1986 has grown into a beloved institution — a place where traditional recipes are preserved and fresh quality is never compromised.

Every product we make carries the warmth of the Lebanese highlands: handcrafted arais, rich labneh, and artisanal cheeses made the old-fashioned way, just as they were always meant to be.
